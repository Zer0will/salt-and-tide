# Local Citation Checklist — Salt & Tide Creative

**Purpose:** Submit consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) to every directory that influences Google's local rankings. **Consistency is the goal — not quantity.**

---

## The canonical NAP block

Use this exact text everywhere. Every character matters. Different abbreviations of the state, city, or phone format will hurt rankings.

```
Salt & Tide Creative
Edmonds, WA 98020
United States
hello@salttidecreative.com
+1 (253) 660-8555
Mon–Fri · 9AM–6PM PT
https://salttidecreative.com
```

**Owner action:** Replace the placeholder phone and email with your real values before submitting anywhere.

## Submission Workflow

For each directory:

1. **Open the directory's "Add a Business" form.**
2. **Paste the canonical NAP block from the top of this document.** Don't retype — paste.
3. **Use the same description on every directory.** Suggested 200-character version:

> Salt & Tide Creative is a boutique web design and digital marketing studio in Edmonds, WA, building websites and SEO strategies for restaurants and small businesses across the Puget Sound.

4. **Categories:** Pick "Web Design" or "Marketing Agency" wherever offered.
5. **Hours:** 9 AM – 6 PM PT, Mon–Fri.
6. **Service area:** Edmonds, Seattle, and surrounding cities (where applicable).
7. **Logo + cover photo:** Use the same files you uploaded to GBP.
8. **Verify:** Most require email verification; some require phone or postcard.
9. **Mark the row checked above.** Maintain this document.

## Pacing

Don't submit all 28 directories in one weekend. Google can interpret a sudden burst of new citations as inauthentic. Suggested pacing:

| Week | Submit |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Tier 1 (#1–8) |
| Week 2 | Tier 2 first half (#9–11) + Tier 4 Edmonds-specific (#22–24) |
| Week 3 | Tier 2 second half (#12–15) + Tier 4 #25–26 |
| Week 4 | Tier 3 (#16–21) |
| Week 5+ | Tier 4 #27–28, plus any niche directories you discover |

This pacing matches how a real local business naturally builds citations over its first month or two.

---

## Maintenance

Citations rot. Phone numbers change, addresses change, hours change. Set a quarterly reminder to:

1. Open each citation
2. Confirm the NAP still matches the canonical block at the top of this document
3. Update any that have drifted

Tools like **Whitespark Local Citation Finder** or **BrightLocal** can automate this audit if you scale beyond manual maintenance. For Year 1, manual is fine.

---

## What NOT to do

- **Don't pay for "automatic" citation services** that submit to 200 spammy directories. They often hurt more than they help.
- **Don't use a different phone number** on different directories. Pick one and stick with it.
- **Don't list a virtual office or PO Box** as your address. Google can detect them.
- **Don't submit to expired/unmaintained directories.** If the directory looks like it hasn't been updated since 2018, skip it.
