# Keyword Map — Salt & Tide Creative

**Last updated:** May 15, 2026
**Owner:** Yael & Co-Founder

This is the master keyword-to-URL assignment for salttidecreative.com. Every public route has been assigned a primary head term and a small cluster of supporting long-tail terms. The site avoids targeting the same head term from multiple URLs (which causes Google to keyword-cannibalize the rankings).

---

## How to read this document

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Route** | The URL path on salttidecreative.com |
| **Primary keyword** | The single term this URL is built to win |
| **Supporting keywords** | The cluster of related terms that share the same intent |
| **Search intent** | Informational (I), Commercial (C), Navigational (N), or Transactional (T) |
| **Difficulty** | Rough competitive intensity — Low / Medium / High |
| **Priority** | Strategic priority for Salt & Tide — H/M/L |

## Intent + Funnel Stage Mapping

The keyword grid maps cleanly to the customer funnel:

| Funnel stage | Owns these routes | What we're targeting |
|---|---|---|
| **Top of funnel (research)** | `/insights/*` | Informational queries from owners who don't yet know they need a website / don't know what one costs |
| **Middle of funnel (consideration)** | `/work`, `/about`, `/seattle-web-design`, `/edmonds-web-design`, `/lynnwood-web-design` | Comparison queries — they know they need help, they're evaluating options |
| **Bottom of funnel (decision)** | `/services`, `/contact` | Commercial-intent queries — they've decided to hire someone, they're picking a vendor |

The Insights articles are designed to capture top-of-funnel traffic and route it through the case studies (proof) into Services + Contact (conversion). Internal linking already enforces this flow.

## Local Pack Strategy

The Edmonds local pack (the three-listing map result Google shows for "web design near me" queries from Edmonds-area searchers) is the single highest-leverage SEO target for the studio. Three things will get you into it:

1. **Google Business Profile** — claimed, verified, complete, and optimized. See `docs/google-business-profile-guide.md`.
2. **NAP consistency** — every directory must show the exact same Name / Address / Phone as the GBP and the site footer. See `docs/local-citation-checklist.md`.
3. **City landing pages** — already shipped: `/seattle-web-design`, `/edmonds-web-design`, `/lynnwood-web-design`. These give Google explicit signal that you serve those cities.

Local pack ranking factors (per industry consensus, May 2026): proximity to searcher (≈25%), prominence/reviews (≈25%), category relevance (≈20%), GBP completeness (≈15%), website signals (≈15%).

## Negative-Keyword Notes

These are terms that *look* relevant but should be deliberately avoided in copy and meta:

- **"Cheap" / "cheapest"** — wrong intent, attracts low-budget tire-kickers, devalues brand.
- **"AI website builder" / "ChatGPT website"** — even though Salt & Tide uses AI in the workflow, these queries are dominated by Wix, Hostinger, and 10Web; not winnable and not your target buyer.
- **"Free website"** — terrible intent.
- **"Best web design agency Seattle"** — list-post intent owned by Clutch and UpCity directories; not winnable as a single agency, better to be on those lists than to fight them.
